Game rules

Joining a game

  1. Choose the 2, 5, or 10 birr game. Each game has 400 unique cartelas.
  2. You may purchase up to five cartelas. Every cartela is charged as a separate stake.
  3. A round needs five different Telegram players. Multiple cartelas from one person still count as one player.
  4. After the fifth player joins, a 20-second countdown begins. No new cartelas can be purchased after the game starts.

How to win

  1. Numbers 1–75 are called randomly without repetition.
  2. A valid Bingo is a complete horizontal row, vertical column, diagonal, or all four corner squares. The centre square is free.
  3. You may mark manually or use Auto Dab. The server’s called-number record is authoritative.
  4. The server verifies every cartela, including during a temporary connection problem.

Prizes, ties and claims

  1. The winner receives the total stakes minus the 5% commission and configured transfer cost.
  2. One to four cartelas completing Bingo on the same first winning call share the prize equally.
  3. Number calling stops immediately when the server detects the first valid Bingo. No additional numbers are called.
  4. The result remains pending for 15 seconds while the server confirms every cartela that won on the same final call.
  5. If more than four cartelas win on the same call, the round is dismissed and all entry stakes are refunded with no commission.
  6. A false Bingo claim blocks only the cartela used for that claim; your other cartelas remain active.
  7. A participating player may dispute the result during the 15-second review. A dispute freezes payment and saves the round as DISPUTED.

Deposits and fair play

  1. Deposit transaction IDs must be unique.
  2. Funds are credited only after an authorized administrator verifies and approves the deposit.
  3. Every result keeps its Game ID, ordered calls, winning cartelas, player information, and exact server timestamps as evidence.
  4. Withdrawals require at least 100 birr. A pending withdrawal is reserved and cannot be spent; rejection releases it and approval completes the debit.
