Privacy notice

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Information used

When you open Lucky through Telegram, the service receives your Telegram user ID and may receive your display name, username, profile photo URL, and language. It also records rooms joined, cards, marks, game results, referral attribution, wallet ledger entries, deposit and withdrawal amounts, payment providers, receiving account details, transaction reference numbers, and administrator approval records.

Why it is used

This information is used to register your player account, operate and secure live games, verify Bingo claims, prevent reuse of deposit references, maintain wallet balances, produce leaderboards, and diagnose service problems. The service does not ask for your Telegram password, banking password, PIN, or one-time login codes.

Storage and sharing

Game data is stored by the Bingo operator. It is not sold. It may be processed by infrastructure providers solely to host and protect the service, or disclosed where legally required. Telegram separately processes information under its own policies.

Your choices

You may stop using the service at any time. To request access, correction, or deletion, contact the operator through the Bingo bot. Some game records may be retained when needed for security, dispute resolution, or legal obligations.

Operator setup

The deploying operator must replace or supplement this notice with its legal name, contact details, retention periods, hosting providers, and jurisdiction-specific disclosures before a public launch.
